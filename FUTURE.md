I like the idea of the physics engine, and would like to expand on it, perhaps
turn it into a full-fledged game.

To make this more original, I could introduce an element of resource management
in simulation time (e.g. there would be resource pipes, which would transfer
resources while the simulation is running, which could then do some special
things (like fueling rockets, or controlling extensible tubes).  The game could
even be some unusual combination of genres, like tower-defense (with resources
being ammunition (or special ammunition) for towers.

There could also be multiple different objective, like get something to the
goal, or prevent something from getting to the goal (a protection level; this
could be the "tower defence" bit). In something like SpaceChem, input resources
may even be controllable in real time by the user.
